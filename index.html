17 SEPTEMBER 2026 · FOR AASTHA

My Aastha,

There are some things I can never explain properly in code. You are one of them.

I still remember that college physics lab moment — when I asked you for a coffee date and somehow ended up saying, “tum coffee pee lena, mai chai lunga.” I don't know whether that was smooth, silly, or both… but I know it became one of those tiny moments that I never want to lose.

And then came 29th June 2025, Shipra Mall — our first date. Since then, you have quietly become one of the most beautiful parts of my everyday thoughts.

Your long black hair with that soft light-brown touch, your deep eyes that feel like an ocean, your beautiful lips… there are details about you that my mind keeps collecting without even trying.

I love your softness, your elegance, your introvert little world, your ambition, your chaotic-funny moments, and your very romantic heart. And yes — even those angry or stressed moments where I start mimicking you and somehow make everything worse before it gets better. 😌

One day, I hope we're somewhere in the valleys of Nepal, looking at mountains together, laughing at old stories, and realizing how many tiny memories it took to reach that view.

Today, I just want you to know this: I am proud of the girl you are, excited about the woman you are becoming, and incredibly grateful that our paths crossed.

Happy birthday, meri jaan. Keep building brilliant things, keep chasing your dreams, keep being wonderfully you — and let me keep annoying you, loving you, and making memories with you along the way.

Always yours, Manan ♡
